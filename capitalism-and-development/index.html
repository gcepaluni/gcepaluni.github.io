Capitalism and Development

Steering Markets Toward Fairness

By Gabriel Cepaluni · Cambridge University Press, 2026 · 321 pp.

Why do some economies turn markets into broad prosperity while others turn them into privilege? The book proposes a framework, Fair Market Institutionalism, that builds on Adam Smith, Milton Friedman, Friedrich Hayek and James Buchanan's constitutional economics, together with the institutionalist work of Douglass North and Daron Acemoglu. It argues that markets create abundance, and that the rules a democracy writes decide who shares in it.
